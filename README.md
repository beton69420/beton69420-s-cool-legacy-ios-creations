# beton’s cool legacy ios creations

a collection of my legacy ios tweaks, fixes, experiments, preservation projects, and random ideas for old iphones, ipads, and ipod touches.

## what this repo is about

- keeping legacy ios devices useful and fun to use.
- restoring broken or abandoned apps and games where possible.
- making quality-of-life tweaks for ios 5, ios 6, and other old ios versions.
- experimenting with old apps, games, jailbreak tweaks, and system behavior.
- documenting useful findings so other legacy ios users can learn from them too.
- building new tools while still keeping the classic ios experience intact.

## current ideas and projects

- legacy essentials, an all-in-one modular tweak for legacy ios.
- quick toggles inspired by old jailbreak utilities.
- fullscreen support for older 3.5-inch iphone apps.
- multitasking and app switcher improvements.
- status bar and battery information tweaks.
- media and youtube compatibility fixes.
- legacy app and game preservation experiments.
- save editing and offline restoration research for abandoned games.
- small utilities for respringing, rebooting, diagnostics, and device information.

## target devices

- iphone 4 and iphone 4s.
- iphone 5.
- ipad 2, ipad 3, and ipad 4.
- ipod touch 4th and 5th generation.
- other legacy ios devices when compatible.

## development status

- very experimental.
- some projects may be unfinished or unstable.
- always back up your device and important files before testing anything.
- compatibility may vary depending on ios version, jailbreak, and device model.

## goals

- make old ios devices more usable in the modern day.
- preserve the feel of classic ios instead of turning it into a modern-looking system.
- keep projects
